# Library

## Adding manga

Open **Browse**, pick a source and look through its Popular and Latest tabs, or search it (with the source's filters). **Global search** searches every source at once. You can also paste a link to a manga from a supported site.

On a manga's page, **Add to library** keeps it, with its chapters, in your library. The cover is saved so the library works offline; right-click a page in the reader and choose **Set as cover** to use another picture.

## Organising

- **Categories** appear as tabs. Create, rename and reorder them in Settings → Library; select manga and use **Set categories** to file them.
- **Display:** comfortable grid, compact grid, covers only or a list, with an adjustable cover size.
- **Filter** by unread, started, downloaded, bookmarked, status or source, and **sort** by title, last read, latest chapter, date added, unread count or total chapters.
- The search box filters the current view; <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> finds any manga in your library.
- **Select** several manga to mark them read or unread, change categories, migrate or remove them.

## Progress and history

Matane remembers the page you stopped on in every chapter. **Continue** on a manga's page opens the next chapter to read; **History** lists what you read recently.

**Incognito mode** (the mask in the title bar) reads without recording anything: no history, progress or statistics.

**Statistics** shows chapters read, reading time, your streak, and favourite genres and sources over the last 30 days, 12 months or all time.

## New chapters

The library checks its manga for new chapters (Settings → Library: how often, and which manga are skipped: completed, not started, or with many unread chapters). New chapters appear on the **Updates** page and as a notification, and can be downloaded by themselves.

## Moving to another source

When a source stops working, **Migrate** (on a manga or a selection) searches other sources for the same title and moves your read chapters, categories and progress over.

## Chapter list

On a manga's page you can filter chapters (unread, downloaded, bookmarked, a scanlation group), sort them, jump to a chapter number and mark the previous chapters as read. Preferred scanlation groups decide which version of a chapter is shown when several groups translated it.
